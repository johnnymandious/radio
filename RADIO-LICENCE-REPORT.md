# Radio licence report

Checked 10 October 2026. 35 albums, 276 tracks, about 38 hours of music. Albums 1–16 were the first batch; albums 17–35 were added the same day.

All music is from the Internet Archive's Netlabels collection. Each album's licence was read from its Internet Archive page (the item's licence field and the release notes). The tracks stream from, and download through, the Internet Archive's own servers. Cover images are resized copies in `assets/radio/covers/`, released with the albums.

## What each licence allows

Every licence below lets anyone copy and redistribute the music in any medium. That covers streaming it on this page and offering it as a download, as long as you:

- **BY (all of them):** credit the artist, name the licence and link to it, and link the source. The player does this for every track, and every download carries it in the MP3 tags (album downloads also include a CREDITS.txt file).
- **NC (NonCommercial):** don't use it commercially. This station has no ads, sales or paywall. If that changes, the NC albums have to come off.
- **ND (NoDerivatives):** don't share a modified version. The audio is never changed. Downloads only get new tag text (credit and licence), which doesn't alter the recording.
- **SA (ShareAlike):** only applies if you remix or adapt the music. The station doesn't, so nothing extra is needed.

### Notes

\* **Particles** and **Time Slice:** the Internet Archive's licence field says CC BY-SA 3.0, but the label's release notes on the same page say CC BY-SA 4.0. Both versions allow streaming and redistribution with credit, so there is no conflict in practice. The station shows 4.0, which is the label's own statement, and the player shows this note.

† **199X:** track 4, "Can You Kiss Me First (synthwave cover)", is a cover of a song by someone else. The artist's CC licence can't cover another writer's composition, so that track is **not** in the station. The other 7 tracks are the artist's own.

‡ **Slow Mission in Space:** the Internet Archive's licence field is blank, but the label's release notes on the page state the licence explicitly ("License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/"). The cover uses a Hubble photo, credited by the label.

§ **Upon Us** (Lost Radiance) and **At the Edge of Solar System** (Vortex Mechanic): same as Particles and Time Slice. The licence field says CC BY-SA 3.0 and the label's notes say 4.0, so the station shows 4.0.

**Time for Love** (Herr Doktor): "Moon Garden (feat. Cavaliers of Fun) (Reprise)" features another act. Enough Records released it under the same licence as the rest of the album, and nothing on the page marks it as a cover.

**Jupiter's Cyclone** (Scott Lawlor & EugeneKha) is about 5 hours long on its own: eight long ambient pieces, some over an hour each.

## Left out, but not because of the licence

- **Vortex Mechanic – Expansive Lives** ([CC BY-SA 4.0](https://archive.org/details/vortex-mechanic-expansive-lives)) has a clear licence, but its cover shows nude figures, so I left it out. It's easy to add back if you want it.
- **Spacebirds – Dreamscape** and **Spacebirds – Galactic Crossroads** ([CC BY-NC-ND 3.0](https://archive.org/details/lv0032010Spacebirds-Dreamscape)): the music's licence is fine, but both covers show Star Trek's USS Enterprise. The artist's licence can't cover Paramount's designs, so I left both albums out. (Spacebirds – Return to Moonbase is in; its cover is original.)
- **Innerspace – Humans as Gods**: CC BY-SA, but its release notes quote *Blade Runner*, and I couldn't confirm the tracks don't sample the film. Left out to be safe.

## Things to know

- **Hosting:** the audio streams from the Internet Archive and isn't copied to this site, so the repository stays small (the covers are about 2 MB in total). If an item is ever taken down, its tracks will fail and the player skips them. Re-check the source links now and then.
- **Licences can't be withdrawn** for copies already released under CC, but a new upload from an artist can change terms. Re-check this report before adding new versions of an album.
- **Low-res cover:** Alpha Trajectory – Sputnik only has a 160 × 160 cover on the Internet Archive.
